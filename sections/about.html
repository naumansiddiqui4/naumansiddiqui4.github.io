About

Building reliable insight from large-scale academic feedback is a deceptively hard problem—one that sits at the intersection of data, systems, and institutional trust. I joined Classranked early and grew alongside the platform, helping shape it from an initial idea into a production system now used by 60 institutions and 300,000+ students, including R1 universities. A core focus has been reducing operational friction—making evaluations configurable in 15 minutes or less while preserving flexibility, performance, and meaningful analysis at scale.

Following Coursedog's acquisition of Classranked, I now lead engineering for Coursedog's next-generation Course Evaluations product as a Staff Engineer, designing it from the ground up for 100+ institutions and 500,000+ students. I partner with Product and the leadership team to shape the roadmap from MVP onward, define the engineering frameworks the team builds on through RFCs, and work across Coursedog's platform teams to turn course evaluation data into a dependable, scalable service institutions can rely on. I also lead how the team works with AI: we build with spec-driven development, use AI skills I introduced to capture screenshots and videos for faster code reviews, and use AI to triage production errors.

My approach to this work is grounded in experience working with data at scale and performing analysis on complex, real-world datasets. During my Master’s in Computer Science at Old Dominion University, I conducted research in the Web Science & Digital Libraries Lab under Dr. Michael L. Nelson and Dr. Michele C. Weigle, where I worked on collecting, analyzing, and visualizing large-scale web and social media datasets. That experience shaped how I reason about data pipelines, performance, and system behavior under scale. Alongside this foundation, graduate study in Project Management informs how I plan, coordinate, and deliver systems that balance technical depth with execution.
